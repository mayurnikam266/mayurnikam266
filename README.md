### ✨ About Me

I think of infrastructure the way a designer thinks of a layout — every pipeline, every subnet, every rolling deployment has to be **clean, intentional, and resilient**. I'm not just spinning up servers; I'm architecting systems that stay calm under pressure. RHCSA, RHCE, and Red Hat Certified in Cloud-native Applications aren't badges on a wall for me — they're proof I've broken things enough times in labs to know how to keep them from breaking in production.

I obsess over the small details most people skip: the rollback plan nobody hopes to use, the IAM policy that's actually least-privilege, the pipeline that fails loud instead of failing silent. That's the mindset I bring to every build.
